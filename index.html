Zinger

The party game of one-liners. One screen for the room, a phone for everyone.

Join a game

Host a game

Open this on a TV, laptop or anything everyone can see. 3 to 8 players join from their phones.

How to play
  1. Everyone gets fill-in-the-blank prompts on their phone. Each prompt goes to two players.
  2. Write the funniest answer you can before time runs out.
  3. Answers go head to head on the big screen. Everyone else votes for the one they like best.
  4. Points are based on your share of the votes. Take every vote for a Shutout bonus.
  5. Round 2 is worth double. Round 3 is the Last Word: one prompt for everyone, and each player gets 3 votes.
